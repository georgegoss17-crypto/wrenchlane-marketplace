Technician service standards

Mobile Automotive Technician Agreement & Service Standards

Important: Technician Agreement. By registering as a Technician, accepting a service request, or performing automotive services through the Platform, you agree to these Technician Terms and Service Standards.

You understand the Platform is a marketplace connecting Customers with independent automotive Technicians. You are responsible for your own workmanship, qualifications, tools, conduct, taxes, licenses, insurance, safety practices, and legal requirements.

You must provide truthful information about years of experience, ASE or manufacturer certifications, dealership or shop experience, specialty training, tools, insurance, licenses, and capabilities. You may not claim credentials you do not possess.

Before accepting a job, you must reasonably evaluate the vehicle, requested service, symptoms, repair location, tools required, expected difficulty, safety requirements, special tools, programming needs, lift needs, and whether the work can be safely performed as mobile service.

You may only perform services authorized by the Customer. You may not begin additional chargeable work until the Customer authorizes it through the Platform when required.

Flat-rate/job-based pricing pays for completion of the authorized service, not the exact clock time. You may not falsely represent actual hours when the Customer is being charged actual hourly labor.

You should document vehicle condition, pre-existing damage, diagnostic findings, significant repair steps, parts, before/after condition, and testing when reasonably possible. This documentation protects both you and the Customer.

You agree to cooperate honestly with disputes and chargebacks by providing relevant records such as authorization, estimates, invoices, before/after photos, notes, messages, parts receipts, timestamps, and completion records.

If the Customer supplies parts, you are not expected to warranty the customer-supplied part itself unless separately agreed or legally required. You remain responsible for properly installing any part you agree to install.

You must not falsify invoices, certifications, diagnoses, photographs, service records, repair times, or customer authorizations. Fraud, theft, unsafe workmanship, intentional misconduct, or serious policy violations may result in suspension or removal.

You acknowledge that Customers choose their Technician, the Platform does not guarantee jobs or income, and you remain responsible for the quality and safety of work you personally perform.
